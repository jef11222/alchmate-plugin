Step 1: 100% Pure Local Standalone Architecture

Unlike web apps that require account registration, cloud sync tokens, or external databases, AlchMate operates as a 100% local, self-contained system running entirely on your PC.

1. Embedded Micro Web Server

The RuneLite plugin runs an embedded micro HTTP server on 127.0.0.1:18833. The full web suite is served straight from your computer with zero latency.

2. Multi-Client Auto-Aggregation

Run 2, 5, or 10 RuneLite clients simultaneously! The first client acts as the Master Hub on port 18833. Other clients automatically act as Spokes and forward events, aggregating into a single dashboard.

3. Zero Cloud / Absolute Privacy

No telemetry, IP tracking, or passwords. Your character names, cash stacks, and session data never leave your machine.

How To Open Your Local Dashboard:

  1. Open RuneLite and click the AlchMate flame icon in the right sidebar.
  2. Click the large 🌐 Open Dashboard button.
  3. Your default browser instantly opens http://127.0.0.1:18833 and brings the window directly to the front!
🌐Direct OSRS Wiki Market Data (No Middlemen)

The browser fetches live Grand Exchange prices, 5-minute trade velocity, and bond pricing directly from the official Old School RuneScape Wiki API (prices.runescape.wiki). Fast, reliable, and completely open.

Step 3: World 308 Bulk Trading & Anti-Scam Guide

Grand Exchange buy limits prevent players from buying more than 70 rune weapons/armor every 4 hours. To alch for 4 to 12 hours a day without stopping, players use player-to-player bulk trading on World 308.

How World 308 Works:

  • Location: Varrock West Bank on World 308 (F2P).
  • Clan Chat: Join Clan Chat as guest: "Varrock Mob" or look for smithers advertising in public chat.
  • Unlimited Volume: 99 Smithers craft thousands of Rune 2h swords, Rune battleaxes, and Adamant platebodies daily. They sell them directly to alchers in noted bulk stacks at GE mid/low price.
  • Zero Grand Exchange Tax: Player-to-player trades have 0% tax, meaning both buyers and sellers make more gold than using the GE!
Crucial Anti-Scam Warning for Bulk Trades:

Always verify the second trade screen carefully! Dishonest traders occasionally attempt to swap noted Rune 2h swords (alch 38,400 gp) for visually identical noted Mithril 2h swords (alch 2,600 gp) or Adamant items. Hover over the noted items and ensure the name text reads "Rune" before accepting!

Step 4: 1-Click RuneLite Bank Tag Generator

Organizing your bank for high alching takes seconds with AlchMate's bank tag exporter:

  1. In the High Alch Suite, add items to your Cart or select top items.
  2. Click the 📋 Copy Bank Tag button in the cart summary bar.
  3. Open RuneLite in-game and make sure the official "Bank Tags" plugin is enabled.
  4. Open your bank, right-click the plus (+) icon at the top of the bank tab bar, and choose "Import Tag Tab".
  5. Press Ctrl + V. RuneLite will create a dedicated "AlchMate" tab containing all your alch items!
